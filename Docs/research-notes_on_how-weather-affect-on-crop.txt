Components used in getting farming weather data:

anemometer: detect wind which is used to determine when to give fertilizer and chemicals using sprays
thermometer: detect temperature which is used to calculate GDD(Growing Degree Days)
Hygrometer: detect humidity in air which is help to determine watering schedule
Barometer: detect atmospheric pressure of air which help to predict weather conditions like rain, storms and all which help to do preventions to protect crop
rain gauge: calculate how much rainfall has been happened which help to watering
pyranometer: calculate solar radiations which help to adjust protection of crop from too much or too little radiations
uv sensor: measures uv rays which help to optimize planting schedules, protect crop from sun damage
leaf wetness sensor: measure surface water which helps to control fungal disease
soil moisture sensor: calculates soils moisture which helps to prevent overwatering or underwatering
soil temperature sensor: calculates soils temperature which help to determine planting timing so soil temperature greatly impect seed germinations and growth rate
