## 2. Institutional Scope & Objectives

The primary academic objectives of this project are:
1. **Applied AI in Agriculture**: Developing deep learning models (EfficientNet-B0 and EfficientNet-B2 architectures) for real-time foliar disease classification across regional Gujarat crops (Cotton, Groundnut, Pepper Bell, Potato, Tomato).
2. **Rural Accessibility & Inclusion**: Eliminating language and literacy barriers through localized speech synthesis (Edge-TTS) and Indic translation in Gujarati and Hindi.
3. **Decoupled Production Architecture**: Demonstrating industry-standard full-stack engineering across FastAPI, React.js, Flutter, and Redis worker queues.
4. **Reliability & Ethical AI**: Incorporating human-in-the-loop agronomic expert review for near-tie and low-confidence predictions.

## 4. Ethical AI Guidelines & Societal Impact

1. **Safety First**: High-risk chemical treatments are grounded in established ICAR/KVK pesticide schedules and require expert human escalation when confidence is below 70%.
2. **Farmer Sovereignty**: Farm boundary geometry and photographic data remain private; zero data is sold to commercial agribusinesses or third-party advertisers.
3. **Open Standards**: REST APIs, GeoJSON geometries, and OpenAPI 3.1 specifications follow open industry standards to ensure interoperability with future public agricultural digital infrastructure.
