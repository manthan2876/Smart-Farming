## 2. Description of the Platform

The Platform is an AI-powered agricultural decision-support and disease diagnostic tool. Capabilities provided include:
1. **Automated Leaf Disease Diagnosis**: Computer vision classification of foliar crop diseases across Cotton, Groundnut, Pepper Bell, Potato, and Tomato.
2. **Pest & Severity Analysis**: Adaptive HSV severity estimation and YOLO-based pest detection.
3. **Agronomic Recommendations**: Advisory grounded in ICAR (Indian Council of Agricultural Research) and KVK guidelines.
4. **Microclimate & Weather Risk Alerts**: Weather forecasts and risk forecasting.
5. **Agricultural Expert Escalation**: Low-confidence diagnosis triage and review by certified agronomy specialists.
